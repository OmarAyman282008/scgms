Performance indicators — documented formulas
  • Meeting compliance — Meetings held ÷ meetings scheduled up to the cut-off date (cancelled excluded)
  • MOM submission compliance — MOMs submitted within the grace period ÷ held meetings whose MOM is due
  • Decision implementation — Actionable decisions implemented and verified ÷ actionable decisions due (target date passed, or closed)
  • Task completion — Tasks verified ÷ tasks due (deadline passed, or verified) — cancelled excluded
  • On-time completion — Tasks verified on or before their deadline ÷ tasks due
  • Decision closure time = average days from a decision's registration to its verified closure. Overdue tasks = open tasks past their deadline. The overall score is the weighted average of the rates above (weights in Settings); it never grows with the number of meetings, decisions or tasks.
