# User guide — دليل المستخدم

The interface opens in Arabic. Use the **English / العربية** button at the top to switch languages; every screen, report and export works in both.

واجهة النظام باللغة العربية افتراضيًا، ويمكن التبديل إلى الإنجليزية من الزر أعلى الصفحة.

---

## The workflow in one picture — دورة العمل

```
الاجتماع ← رفع المحضر ← اعتماد المحضر ← تسجيل القرارات ← إسناد المهام ← الإنجاز والأدلة ← التحقق ← الإغلاق والأرشفة
Meeting → MOM submission → MOM approval → Decision registration → Task assignment → Progress & evidence → Verification → Closure & archive
```

Three rules hold throughout:

* **A task is never "done" by its owner.** It is closed only after the reviewer checks the uploaded evidence.
* **A decision closes only when all of its tasks are verified.**
* **A MOM is archived when its decisions are closed.**

## Dean and Vice-Dean — العميد ووكيل الكلية

**Executive Overview — النظرة التنفيذية العامة.** The KPI tiles and charts cover all committees. Use the filter bar to narrow by academic year, semester, committee, dates, priority, responsible person or task status. Click a bar or tile to open the details behind it.

**Management Follow-Up Center — مركز المتابعة الإدارية.** Answers *what needs my attention today, who is responsible, and what is blocking it*. Its tabs are:

* Action Required — إجراءات مطلوبة
* Urgent Tasks — المهام العاجلة
* Pending Decisions — القرارات المعلقة
* Overdue Items — البنود المتأخرة
* Pending Verification — بانتظار التحقق
* MOM Approvals — اعتمادات المحاضر
* Escalations — التصعيدات
* Recently Closed — أغلقت مؤخرًا

Extension and cancellation requests are decided from here or from the task page.

**Assign an urgent task — إسناد مهمة عاجلة.** Use the red button at the top of every page. Choose the person or people, deadline, expected deliverables and (optionally) the committee and source, such as a Dean's directive or a university council decision. The task gets an `MGT-` code and appears in the Follow-Up Center. It is verified by management, not by the committee chair.

**Final MOM approval — الاعتماد النهائي للمحضر.** After the chair approves, the MOM appears under *MOMs & Approvals → Waiting for my approval*. Open it, read the file, and either **Approve** or **Return for revision** with a comment. Approval locks the file.

**Reports — التقارير.** There are eight reports, each exportable to PDF or Excel with the same filters.

## Committee chair — رئيس اللجنة

* **Review the MOM** uploaded by the secretary: approve it, or return it with what must change. If you upload the MOM yourself, your approval is applied automatically.
* **Register decisions** from the approved MOM, or let the secretary do it, from the meeting's *Decisions & Action Items* tab.
* **Convert each decision into tasks — تحويل القرار إلى مهام.** The decision's details are pre-filled. Choose the responsible member(s), the deadline and the expected evidence, then click **Confirm and assign**.
* **Verify submitted work — التحقق من الإنجاز.** Under *My Dashboard → Waiting for my verification*, open the task, check the evidence and either **Approve evidence — verify and close** or **Return for more work** with what is missing. You cannot verify a task assigned to yourself; management verifies it.
* **Extension requests** from members arrive at you. Approve or reject them on the task page.

## Committee secretary — أمين اللجنة

1. **Schedule the meeting — جدولة اجتماع:** *Committee Meetings → Schedule meeting*, or generate the regular monthly meetings for the year from the committee page. Invitations go to all members.
2. **Download the template — تحميل نموذج محضر الاجتماع المعتمد:** on the meeting page, *Minutes (MOM)* tab. The file is pre-filled with the committee, number, date, time, venue, members, agenda and the open decisions from previous meetings.
3. **Record attendance — الحضور** after the meeting.
4. **Upload the completed minutes — رفع محضر الاجتماع بعد الاستكمال:** a signed PDF or DOCX, within the allowed days (7 by default). If the minutes are returned, fix them and upload again; earlier versions are kept.
5. **Register the decisions** once the minutes are approved.

The *Details & agenda* tab of every meeting lists **pending decisions from previous meetings — قرارات معلقة من اجتماعات سابقة** so they can be followed up in the agenda.

## Committee member — عضو اللجنة

*My Dashboard — لوحتي* shows your open tasks, upcoming meetings, your committees and unread notifications.

On a task page, under **Update this task — تحديث هذه المهمة**:

| Tab | Use |
|---|---|
| Progress — الإنجاز | Set the % complete and describe what was done. Tick *Report an obstacle* if you are blocked; the chair is alerted. |
| Upload evidence — رفع دليل | Attach the files that prove completion: report, screenshot, signed document, minutes. |
| Submit for verification — تقديم للتحقق | Available once at least one evidence file is attached. The reviewer is notified. |
| Extension / obstacle — تمديد / عائق | Request a new deadline with a reason, or escalate to management if something outside the committee is blocking you. |

If a task is **returned — أعيدت للاستكمال**, read the reviewer's comment, upload the additional evidence and submit again.

## Notifications — الإشعارات

The bell shows unread notifications. These include:

* meeting invitations and reminders;
* MOMs to upload or approve;
* new and urgent tasks;
* approaching and passed deadlines;
* returned or verified work;
* decisions closed;
* management comments;
* extension decisions;
* escalations.

In **My profile — ملفي الشخصي** you can also choose to receive them by e-mail, immediately or as one daily digest (once e-mail is enabled by the administration).

## Digital Filing Archive — الأرشيف الرقمي

Every file is linked to its committee, meeting, decision or task. Browse by academic year → committee → folder:

* Formation — التشكيل
* Action Plan — خطة العمل
* Meetings — الاجتماعات
* Approved MOM — المحضر المعتمد
* Decisions — القرارات
* Action Items — بنود العمل
* Evidence — أدلة التنفيذ
* Periodic and Annual Reports — التقارير الدورية والسنوية

You can also search by title, file name or decision ID.

* Approved and verified records are **locked**: they can be viewed and downloaded but not replaced.
* Other documents keep every uploaded **version**.
* Documents marked **confidential — سري** are visible to the Dean and Vice-Dean only.
* **Export committee records (ZIP)** downloads a committee's whole file with a CSV register.
